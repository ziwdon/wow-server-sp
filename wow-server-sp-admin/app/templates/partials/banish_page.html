Banish

A one-shot spell for raid bosses that need a full raid to kill. On this small server most 25- and 40-player encounters cannot be completed as designed, so a chosen character can learn Banish and end the fight in one hit.

Banish instantly kills the targeted hostile or neutral NPC and rewards it as a normal kill: loot, kill credit and achievements for the caster and group. It cannot target players, bots or friendly NPCs, costs nothing, casts instantly and has only the global cooldown. Once taught it appears in the spellbook's Blizzard tab as “Banish” from the next login.

How to remove
  1. Forget Banish from every character that knows it (below), or in MySQL: DELETE FROM acore_characters.character_spell WHERE spell = 35182;
  2. Delete the definition rows: DELETE FROM acore_world.spell_dbc WHERE ID = 35182; and DELETE FROM acore_world.conditions WHERE SourceTypeOrReferenceId = 17 AND SourceEntry = 35182;
  3. Restart the worldserver (Dashboard → Restart).

Keep this order: a character that still knows 35182 once the rows are gone gets the client's harmless original test spell instead.
